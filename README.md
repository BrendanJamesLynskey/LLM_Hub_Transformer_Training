# Transformer Training

How a decoder-only transformer is pretrained, end to end: the loop and the loss, data and tokens, backpropagation, optimisers, learning rate and batch size, initialisation and normalisation, stability, mixed precision, memory, data and model parallelism, training over slow links, scaling laws and running a real job, with a bridge to fine-tuning.

**Live index:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/LLM_Hub_Transformer_Training/

The thirteen decks are the slide companion to **Part 2 (Training) of [Transformer Decoder Explained](https://transformer-training-explained.vercel.app/training)**, sixteen animated chapters in which Part 1's 5,376-parameter decoder trains in the browser. Every measured number in the decks comes from the site's [results.md](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/transformer-explainer/blob/main/results.md); everything else is cited. The toy model shows mechanisms; for real measurements of training over slow links, see the [Compute in Transit results site](https://compute-in-transit-results.vercel.app).
